How this is laid out

Each row is one cycle of the 20 s skills (16 s with alacrity), starting at the whirl with the bomb burst. Each column is one mini loop, from one Electro-whirl to the next: bomb burst, nade filler, other filler. In cycle 0 the opener takes the bomb burst column. Electro-whirl and Shrapnel Grenade are one block. Hammer autos in a row are one block, with the number of auto casts on it.

A skill without an outline is in the same mini loop in at least three quarters of the full cycles. A dashed blue outline marks a floating skill: it moves between mini loops with its own cooldown. A faded, dashed red icon is a usual skill this cycle left out. Small icons above a skill are instants cast during it (or right before it): detonations and Bloodstone Fervor (red ring). The width of an auto block is its number of auto casts.

Kit swaps and other instants are not listed. The bench log is dps.report 55xd-20260422-192633_golem. Cooldowns are the GW2 API's, ready 25% sooner under alacrity and counted from the end of the cast.
